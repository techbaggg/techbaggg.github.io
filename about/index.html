About Jagdish Krishanlal Arora

A wide-ranging body of work connecting storytelling, knowledge, exploration and technology.

Jagdish Krishanlal Arora writes across genres, bringing together accessible ideas, speculative questions, history, spirituality and imaginative storytelling.

His interests span the ancient and the futuristic: sacred places and lost civilizations, time travel and space adventure, education and motivation, artificial intelligence, and stories inspired by history.

This website is the central home for his author profile, selected books and AI/data experience.
