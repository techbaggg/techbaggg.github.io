ABOUT THE AUTHOR

About Jagdish Krishanlal Arora

A wide-ranging body of work connecting storytelling, knowledge, exploration and technology.

Jagdish Krishanlal Arora writes across genres, bringing together accessible ideas, speculative questions, history, spirituality and imaginative storytelling.

His interests span the ancient and the futuristic: sacred places and lost civilizations, time travel and space adventure, education and motivation, artificial intelligence, and stories inspired by history.

Books and writing

His catalog covers fiction, children's literature, education, mathematics, science, travel, spirituality, history, motivation and speculative subjects. Some books are designed to explain ideas in an accessible way, while others use fiction and adventure to explore imaginative possibilities.

The website includes individual book pages where available, together with links to the corresponding Amazon editions. Book subjects and descriptions vary by title, so readers can use the catalog to explore different areas of the author's work.

Artificial intelligence and data

Alongside writing, the site documents practical work and interests in artificial intelligence and data workflows. These areas include large language model evaluation, prompt evaluation, data annotation, dataset quality review, speech and multilingual data, multimodal AI, conversational AI and model error analysis.

The AI and data section focuses on practical quality and evaluation activities rather than presenting a single narrow specialization. It describes the types of tasks and workflows represented on the site.
