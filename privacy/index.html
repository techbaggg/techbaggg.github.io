Privacy Policy

This page explains the basic privacy practices for the official website of Jagdish Krishanlal Arora. The site is a static informational and author website.

This website does not require visitors to create an account. Contact information is only used when a visitor voluntarily sends a message by email or through the contact form. The site may use analytics services, including GoatCounter and Metricool, to understand website traffic and improve the site. These services may process technical information such as pages viewed, referrer information, browser or device information, and approximate visit activity according to their own policies.

The site may contain links to external websites, including Amazon. External sites have their own privacy practices and policies, which are outside this website's control.

No payment information is collected directly by this website. Visitors should avoid sending passwords, financial information, or other sensitive information through ordinary email or contact forms.

If you have a privacy question, contact the site owner through the contact page.
