ABOUT THE AUTHOR

A wide-ranging body of work.

Jagdish Krishanlal Arora writes across genres, bringing together accessible ideas, speculative questions, history, spirituality and imaginative storytelling. His portfolio includes books for general readers as well as works centered on emerging technology and big questions about humanity.

His interests span the ancient and the futuristic: sacred places and lost civilizations, time travel and space adventure, motivation and education, artificial intelligence, and stories inspired by major moments in history.
