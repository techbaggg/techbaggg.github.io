ADVENTURE • MYSTERY

Secrets of Mount Kailash, Bermuda Triangle and the Lost City of Atlantis

A journey through legendary places, enduring mysteries and the stories that surround Mount Kailash, the Bermuda Triangle and Atlantis.

Secrets of Mount Kailash, Bermuda Triangle and the Lost City of Atlantis brings together three subjects that have generated generations of curiosity: Mount Kailash, the Bermuda Triangle and the lost city of Atlantis.

Each subject occupies a different place in history, geography, legend and popular culture. Bringing them together creates an opportunity to look at the way mysterious places and unresolved questions become part of the stories people tell about the world.

Mount Kailash

Mount Kailash is a place of profound cultural and spiritual significance. Its importance extends beyond its physical geography, making it a natural subject for readers interested in sacred places, pilgrimage, history and the relationship between landscape and belief.

The Bermuda Triangle

The Bermuda Triangle has become one of the best-known modern mystery subjects. Stories about unusual disappearances and unexplained events have inspired books, documentaries and continuing debate. Exploring the subject provides a way to consider the difference between documented events, interpretation and the legends that develop around them.

The Lost City of Atlantis

Atlantis represents another enduring mystery: the idea of a lost civilization remembered through ancient writing and centuries of speculation. Its continuing appeal comes from the intersection of history, archaeology, philosophy, imagination and the possibility of discovering something believed to have disappeared.

For readers who enjoy mystery and exploration

The book is suited to readers who are drawn to legendary locations, ancient mysteries, travel, spirituality and questions that invite further investigation. It reflects the adventurous and exploratory side of Jagdish Krishanlal Arora’s writing, where history and popular mystery provide starting points for curiosity.
