SCIENCE FICTION

Time Travel

A speculative exploration of one of humanity’s most enduring questions: can time itself become a destination?

Time Travel is a science-fiction work by Jagdish Krishanlal Arora built around a concept that has fascinated writers, scientists and readers for generations: the possibility of moving beyond the ordinary experience of past, present and future.

Time travel is more than a mechanism for reaching another date. It creates a way to think about cause and effect, memory, choice, discovery and the consequences of seeing events from a different point in time. Those questions make the subject naturally suited to speculative storytelling, where an imagined possibility can become a starting point for exploring much larger ideas.

What readers can explore

The book is aimed at readers who enjoy science fiction, futuristic ideas and stories that begin with a simple question and follow it into unfamiliar territory. Its central subject opens space for reflection on how people understand history, the present moment and the future they are moving toward.

For readers discovering Jagdish Arora’s work for the first time, Time Travel represents the speculative side of his writing. It sits alongside his interests in science, space, technology and imaginative adventure while remaining focused on the enduring fascination of time itself.

Why time travel remains compelling

The idea of time travel invites questions that do not have to be limited to technical explanations. What would a person learn by encountering another era? How might knowledge change the way an event is understood? What responsibilities would accompany the ability to cross time? Science fiction gives readers room to consider these possibilities without requiring every imagined scenario to be treated as a prediction.
