ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Large Language Models

An accessible entry point into artificial intelligence, language technology and machine intelligence.

Large Language Models is a book by Jagdish Krishanlal Arora focused on one of the most important developments in contemporary artificial intelligence: systems that can process and generate human language at scale.

Large language models have changed how people think about search, writing, conversation, translation, programming and many other forms of interaction with computers. Understanding them requires more than knowing that an AI can produce text. It also means asking how these systems are trained, how their outputs should be evaluated, where errors arise and what responsible use looks like.

A reader-friendly introduction to AI

The book connects with Arora’s wider interest in accessible explanations of complex subjects. Readers interested in artificial intelligence can use this page as a starting point for exploring the ideas surrounding language models, machine intelligence and the changing relationship between people and technology.

Large language models are especially interesting because their strengths and limitations appear directly in everyday interaction. A model may produce fluent language while still making factual mistakes, misunderstanding context or following an instruction imperfectly. That makes evaluation and careful human judgment important parts of working with AI.

From language models to practical AI work

Arora’s broader AI and data experience includes LLM evaluation, response rating, prompt engineering, dataset annotation, dataset quality review, multilingual and code-switching data, multimodal AI, conversational AI testing and model error analysis. These areas provide practical context for readers who want to connect the concept of an LLM with the workflows used to evaluate and improve AI systems.

Whether you are approaching AI from a technology, education or general-interest perspective, the subject of large language models offers a useful way to examine how machine intelligence is developing and how people can interact with it thoughtfully.
