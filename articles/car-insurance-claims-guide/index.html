CAR INSURANCE CLAIMS

Car Insurance Claims: A Practical Guide After an Accident

By Jagdish Krishanlal Arora

After a vehicle accident, the insurance claim process can feel complicated because several tasks happen at once: documenting what happened, protecting people from further harm, contacting the relevant insurer and understanding what the policy covers. The exact process varies by country, insurer and policy, so this guide is a general starting point rather than legal or insurance advice.

Safety comes first

If an accident has caused injuries or an immediate hazard, address emergency needs first. Follow local requirements for reporting serious accidents and avoid putting yourself in danger while collecting information.

Document the incident

Where it is safe and lawful to do so, record the date, time, location, vehicle positions, visible damage and relevant road conditions. Keep photographs and copies of documents. Contact details for other parties and witnesses can also be useful.

Notify the insurer

Policies commonly contain requirements about reporting accidents promptly. Review your policy and contact the insurer through its official claims channel. Give accurate factual information and keep a record of claim numbers, correspondence and submitted documents.

Understand the process

An insurer may investigate the circumstances, assess damage and determine coverage under the applicable policy. Timelines and procedures vary. If there is a disagreement about coverage or liability, review the policy wording and consider appropriate professional or regulatory assistance.
