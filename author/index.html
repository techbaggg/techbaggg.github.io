AUTHOR PROFILE

Jagdish Krishanlal Arora

Jagdish Krishanlal Arora is an author, storyteller and AI professional whose interests span books, science, history, spirituality, travel and emerging technology.

His published work explores imaginative questions and accessible ideas across different subjects and genres. His author portfolio includes speculative themes such as time travel, mysteries surrounding legendary places, travel writing and artificial intelligence.

He also has practical experience with modern AI workflows including LLM evaluation, prompt evaluation, data annotation, dataset quality review, speech and multilingual data, multimodal AI, conversational AI testing and model error analysis.
